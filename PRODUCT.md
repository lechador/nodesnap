## Platform

web

## Users
Homeowners describing and exploring a home; professionals reviewing the resulting model and evidence. Confirmed by the founder on 31 October 2026: architecture and chat lead, rather than a dense CAD workspace.

## Product Purpose
Turn text and voice into an editable architectural model, coordinated drawings, deterministic calculations, and a revision-bound professional review package.

## Operating Context
Desktop-first architectural studio; mobile preserves conversation, model review and document access. Start a separate building from a text or voice brief, reopen a saved building, or explicitly explore the reproducible synthetic NYC villa. New buildings have their own footprint, floor and room program, with optional basement, pool and occupied roof terrace. The six-room-per-floor villa is an optional example, not the template for new designs.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The canonical Python model owns geometry and SI quantities. Typed commands, immutable revisions, stable entity IDs and explicit source basis are mandatory. Preserve all requested engineering disciplines and show unresolved methods honestly. Runtime AI is Meta-only and server-side. Keep existing Linode Docker Compose deployment and service topology unchanged. Never imply permit approval or construction readiness.

Whole-building generation currently supports rectangular 8–60 m footprints, 1–12 above-ground floors and up to 20 requested rooms per floor, with connected deterministic room layouts. Whole-program redesigns remain reversible revisions. Explicit dimensions and area targets are protected during bounded proposal repair; inferred quantities are labeled demonstration assumptions. This is a concept generator, not an unrestricted CAD or code-compliant building-design service.

## Brand Commitments
NodeSnap. Founder requests a complete visual replacement, with Archicad-quality architectural 3D, precise 2D plans and expressive building/chat animations. Chief Architect, the supplied modern-villa reference and LinkedIn building workflow define craft expectations. Specification calls for charcoal navigation, light drawing canvas, refined stone/timber/metal/glass and one calm accent.
